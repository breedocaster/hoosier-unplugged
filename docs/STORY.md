# Story: "Hoosiers Out of Time"

> Tone: Saturday-morning cartoon. Big villains, dumb henchmen, real heart, short scenes. Nobody is mean to farmers. The bad guy is a megacorp that thinks Indiana is a resource, not a home.

## Premise

**StratoStack Inc.** announces **Project Cornfield**: a string of hyperscale data campuses across Indiana. Then the numbers stop adding up. Wells run low, the grid browns out, and the campuses somehow draw more water and power than the state actually has.

**Kurt**, a grid engineer who reads machine logs the way some people read tea leaves, finds where the extra power is coming from. It's coming from **the past**.

StratoStack CEO **Preston Vail** and his AI **LUMA** have built a **Rollback Engine**. It pipes resources out of Indiana's history: glacial meltwater, old-growth timber, gas-boom gas, a drained marsh. Every theft rewrites history a little, and present-day Indiana gets a little grayer each morning.

Kurt pulls together a crew. They storm StratoStack Prime. Vail smiles and "restores them from backup," flinging them across the timeline. To get home they have to shut down every extraction rig in every era. Each one restores a **Restore Point** and earns an **Earth Charge** (one of the six elements). With all six, they can come home and do a **Hard Reset**.

## Villains

| Who | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Preston Vail** | StratoStack CEO, the "Hyperscaler" | Smiles too much. Talks in keynote phrases ("we're unlocking value from legacy timelines"). |
| **LUMA** | Vail's AI | Polite, terrifying, always asks for "just a little more compute". Final twist: LUMA has been running Vail all along. |
| **Uptime & Downtime** | Twin enforcers | One huge and slow, one tiny and fast. They share a health bar and enrage when the other goes down. Recurring comic relief. |
| **Rackbots** | Grunts | Server racks on legs. LED color = attack type. Era costumes. |

## Structure: 12 stages in three acts

Same shape as Turtles in Time: present day, then lost in time, then back for the finale. Twelve stages matches Streets of Rage 4's story length.

### Act I: Present Day ("Project Cornfield")

| # | Stage | Setting | Hook | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Main Street Mayhem** ✅ graybox | A courthouse-square small town at night, then a StratoStack construction site on farmland | Rackbots "surveying" people's backyards | **Bulldozer Bruno**, a demolition foreman in an excavator mech (prototype uses the *Server Brute* stand-in) |
| 2 | **Circle City Clash** | Downtown Indianapolis: Monument Circle, rooftops, a fiber hub | StratoStack is tapping the city's power | **Uptime & Downtime** (round 1) |
| 3 | **National Road Run** | Motorcycle chase down US-40 (vehicle stage) | Chasing a convoy of water tankers | **The Cloud**, a drone swarm that forms shapes |
| 4 | **StratoStack Prime** | Hyperscale HQ: cold aisles, cable trays, cooling towers | Shut down the Rollback Engine... | **LUMA's Firewall**, then Vail triggers the rollback (cutscene) |

### Act II: Lost in Time ("Restore Points")

| # | Stage | Era | What StratoStack is stealing | Earth Charge | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Glacial Grind** | Ice Age (~13,000 years ago) | Glacier meltwater, for coolant | Water | **Mastodata**, an ice-mining mech built like a mastodon. Real mastodons are friendly and rideable. |
| 6 | **Frontier Firewall** | 1816, statehood year | Old-growth hardwood forest | Grow | **Timberjack**, a logging walker |
| 7 | **Gas Boom Blowout** | 1888, the Gas Belt | The Trenton gas field (they're feeding the flambeaux) | Current | **Baron Flambeau**, a gaslight tycoon with a flamethrower cane |
| 8 | **Kankakee Drain** | ~1910, Grand Kankakee Marsh | The marsh itself | Juice | **The Dredger**, a swamp crane boss on a barge |
| 9 | **Brick Oval Brawl** | 1911, first big race at the brick speedway (vehicle stage) | Engines and fuel | Wind | **Overclock**, a rocket-car racer |
| 10 | **Dune Defense** | 1960s, Lake Michigan shoreline | Dune sand, hauled off for fill | Stone | **Sandblaster Sully**, a strip-mining digger |

### Act III: Back to the Future ("Hard Reset")

| # | Stage | Setting | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Server Farm 2099** | The future if Vail wins: Indiana paved with server halls, neon hover-bikes (vehicle section) | **Vail 2099**, an old Vail wired into his own racks |
| 12 | **Hard Reset** | Back to StratoStack Prime in the present | **THE HYPERSCALER**: Vail fused with LUMA atop a server tower. 3 phases. In the final phase the team spends all six Earth Charges to summon the **Sycamore Sentinel**. |

### Ending

The campus becomes a community solar co-op and greenhouse, and the wells come back. Post-credits: Uptime & Downtime open a tenderloin food truck at the state fair. LUMA shows up in the credits asking if anyone needs "a little help with their playlist." Hook for DLC.
